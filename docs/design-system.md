# TIJRA Design System

## Product UI principles
- Arabic-first RTL layouts with thumb-friendly mobile interactions.
- High information density without visual clutter.
- Hierarchy: business status → primary action → exceptions → detail.
- Smart actions are explainable: every recommendation shows why and expected saving/impact.
- Neutral platform language: no financing and no delivery management.

## Visual language
- Premium commerce/operations aesthetic inspired by modern 21st.dev interaction patterns.
- Layered surfaces, soft borders, restrained gradients, subtle spotlight effects and short motion.
- Cards are functional containers, not decoration.
- Numbers and deltas have stronger hierarchy than labels.
- Status accents are semantic and consistent.

## Interaction patterns
- Global command palette (Cmd/Ctrl + K) for fast navigation.
- Sticky, reachable mobile navigation.
- Notification center for Smart Price and inventory alerts.
- Hover/focus feedback on desktop without making mobile dependent on hover.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected.
- Empty/error/loading/success states stay explicit.

## Smart Price
- Compare identical products across suppliers.
- Show compared price, best price, saving per unit, saving percentage and estimated saving on suggested quantity.
- Never imply unit-price alone is the whole decision when MOQ/availability changes the choice.
