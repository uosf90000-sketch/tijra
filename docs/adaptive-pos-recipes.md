# Adaptive POS and food recipes

- Recipes/components are available only for RESTAURANT and CAFE businesses.
- Grocery POS prioritizes barcode scanning.
- Restaurant/cafe POS prioritizes product images and hides base recipe details from cashiers.
- Hardware/parts POS prioritizes part-number search and available quantity.
- Owners configure food product ingredients and optional extras; cashiers only see sellable extras.
- Recipe APIs and product sale-mode APIs enforce the same activity restrictions server-side.
